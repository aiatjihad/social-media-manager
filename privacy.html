Privacy Policy

SocialHub stores the content you create and the authorization tokens needed to publish to the social accounts you choose to connect. Tokens are kept on our backend server and are never written into the public website.

  • We use your account data only to publish the content you ask us to publish.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can disconnect an account at any time; this removes its stored token from your browser.

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub repository.
